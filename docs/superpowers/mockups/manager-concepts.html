Agent manager three ideas of our own, pick one to build

Sample data, not your agents

A · Gravity

You are the light in the middle. Every agent is a small world that orbits you. How close it is shows how much it needs you: agents waiting for you sink to the inner ring and pull in further the longer they wait, sending pulses towards you. Working agents circle fast and leave a trail. Resting agents drift slowly at the edge and fade.

Colour = tool (coral Claude Code, blue Codex, violet Copilot). Hover for a name, click for details.

Real today: who waits, works or rests, wait time, tool, project, model, tool calls, plan.

B · Rivers of work

Time flows left to right, ending at now. Each agent is a river: wide when it is busy, thin when it is quiet, a dotted trickle when it rests. Sparks float along it with the work. A waiting agent is a dammed river: it stops, and an amber knot at "now" grows with every minute it waits.

You see in one look what happened in the last two hours, not only what is happening now. Click a river for details.

Needs a new piece: the service would keep a short history of each agent's activity (planned).

C · Crew deck

Every agent is a card you can hold. Cards tilt towards your mouse like real ones. A card that needs you knocks: it hops up now and then, glowing amber. Resting cards go grey. Click a card to flip it over for the details.

The emblem on each card is drawn from its name, so an agent always keeps its own look.

Real today: everything on the cards.
