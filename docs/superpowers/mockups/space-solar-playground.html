My answer on the sun

A static, real sun is better recommended

If the sun were the current project, it would swap every time you pick another project and the whole system would reshuffle. A fixed sun keeps the system stable: you learn where each project lives, and picking one just moves the "YOU ARE HERE" mark.

What the sun is
This computer. Every agent the hub watches runs on it, so it is the one thing all projects truly share. That makes it honest, not just pretty.
What it shows
Nothing invented. It only flares more often when many agents are working at once (try "Everyone busy"), and the flare is decoration.
The current project
A planet you are standing on: bigger, with a white bracket and its orbit lit. Open it and you are in the hall, as today.
