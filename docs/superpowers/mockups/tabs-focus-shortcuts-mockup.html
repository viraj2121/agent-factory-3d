2. Focus modes

One button (or F to cycle, 1–4 with Shift to pick) changes how much is on screen. Picked in a small menu at the bottom, saved in the settings file.

Overview

Today's look
  • Project bar, toolbar, all labels
  • Idle tour on

Crew

See who is doing what
  • Crew list docked on the right, all agents of the project
  • Name tags on every robot, also from afar
  • Decoration (weather, belts) calmer

Needs you

Only what waits for you
  • Camera flies to the bay that needs you
  • Everything else dimmed
  • Next waiting agent: N

Cinema

For showing it off
  • No bars, no labels, no counter
  • Tour on, sound follows the camera
  • Any key except Esc keeps it; Esc leaves

4. Space themes and space weather (instead of rain)

The hub floats in space, so clouds and rain do not belong. Two separate choices in Settings: a theme (the look of the sky and the light) and space weather (what moves outside the dome). Weather still follows the project's real status, as rain does now, and stays decoration.

Deep space

Default
  • Black-blue sky, sharp stars, a distant planet
  • Cool white station light

Nebula

Colourful
  • Slow drifting purple and teal gas clouds
  • Islands lit by the nebula's colour

Solar orbit

Warm
  • A big sun on one side, long warm shadows
  • Matches today's orange panels

Event horizon

Dramatic
  • A black hole with a glowing ring behind the hub
  • Stars bend slightly near it

Aurora planet

Calm
  • Above a planet's night side, green aurora curtains
  • Softest look, good for long hours

Space weather, driven by the project's real status

Calm drift project idle

Slow dust motes and a few small asteroids drifting past. A shooting star now and then.

Meteor shower agents working

Streaks of light across the sky, faster and more of them the busier the project is. Takes the place of rain.

Ion storm needs you

Purple-blue arcs crawl over the shield dome, with a low crackle when sound is on. Takes the place of lightning and thunder.

Solar flare an agent failed

A short orange flash washes over the islands. Only when an agent reports an error.

Comet plan finished

A comet with a long tail crosses the sky once when a project's own task list reaches 100%.

Off any time

Clean sky, nothing moving. Lite quality picks this by itself.

Honest data: calm, meteor and ion storm follow the status the hub already shows. Solar flare and comet need data the service does not read yet (agent errors, a finished plan as an event), so they stay off until that exists.
