Sorting the controls one row of everything → six clear homes

Today one bottom bar holds seven different kinds of thing: places to fly to, the level switch, panels, toggles, settings, help text and the quality chip. The idea below gives each kind its own corner of the screen, and the bottom of the screen becomes free so the 3D view is not covered. Click around: the icons toggle, the map dots fly (here they just light up), the right-hand tabs open panels, and ? opens the shortcut sheet.

Where each kind of control goes

places Mini-map, bottom left

A small top-down drawing of the hall. The core, the four bays, the studio, the launch pad, the library and the cart are dots; click one to fly there, hover to see its name. It replaces six text buttons, and it shows where things are, which words cannot.

level Hall | System, top centre

The two levels are a switch, not a button among others, so they sit next to the LIVE banner as a two-way control. It also shows where you are.

toggles Icon row, top right

Sound, tour, picture mode and settings are things you switch on or off, so they are small icons that light up when on, with a tooltip and the key. Quality becomes a setting; it only appears next to them as a chip when you are not on Auto.

panels Tab strip, right edge

Project info, the crew list, the library (the project's markdown files) and recent activity are tabs on the right edge. One click opens the panel beside it; the same place for every panel, instead of buttons scattered about.

projects Rail, left

Stays as it is. It is the one thing that is about which project, not how you look.

help ? button and a fading hint

"Drag to turn · wheel to zoom · right-drag to move" shows once for a few seconds, then fades. All keys live on the ? sheet. The bottom of the screen is empty again.

Two other options

B · Command palette

Press Ctrl+K and type: "studio", "sound", "settings", "ledger api". Nothing is on screen until you ask. Great for people who live on the keyboard, but nobody discovers it by looking, so it works best with the proposal above, not instead of it.

C · Radial menu on right-click

Right-click in empty space and a ring of places opens around the cursor. Very fast and uses no permanent space. It conflicts with right-drag to move the view today, so it would need a different key (hold Space).

My recommendation: the proposal (mini-map, level switch, icon row, right tabs, help sheet), with the command palette added later. Nothing is built yet; tell me what to change.
